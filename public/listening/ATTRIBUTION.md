# Cambridge public listening sample transcripts

The two PDF files in this directory are unmodified public listening sample transcripts published by Cambridge English. Copyright remains with Cambridge University Press & Assessment. They are not covered by this application's MIT licence.

The local copies allow the learner-requested download/import operation to work without a cross-origin PDF request. Audio remains streamed directly from Cambridge; no audio recordings are copied into this repository.

Source page, checked 2026-09-10:
https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/qualifications/preliminary/preparation/
